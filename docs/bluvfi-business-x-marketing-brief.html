Status update: 13 November 2026

The AI has judgment, never authority.

1. Positioning

One line. Bluvfi Business is the trust layer for AI agents that pay invoices: the agent reads and proposes, your rules and your council decide, and every decision leaves evidence anyone can check.

Who it is for

  • Teams that want an AI agent doing accounts payable but will not hand it a wallet.
  • DAOs and crypto-native companies that already hold a treasury on NEAR (a Sputnik DAO, as Trezu runs).
  • Auditors and finance leads who need to see why a payment was made, not just that it was.

What it is, and what it is not

It isIt is not
A policy gate, fraud checks and a council vote between an AI's opinion and any money moving.An AI that controls funds. The agent's key can propose a payment and nothing else.
Built on a NEAR DAO treasury the business already controls. No smart contracts of our own.A custodian. Funds do not sit with us.
Evidence by default: the policy version, the AI's signed review, the checks that ran, who approved.A promise to "trust the AI". Nothing here asks for trust it can show instead.
One app for the business's money: invoices, bills, stocks, rupee transfers, prices and an agent to ask.Investment advice, a bank, or a broker. Providers do those parts, in the business's own accounts.

4. Feature posts

One feature, one post, one screenshot. Check section 5 before editing any of these.

Demo and live

Every business in Bluvfi Business has a Demo | Live switch. Demo runs on a simulated DAO with no keys and no funds, and keeps its own records. Live uses the real chain and your own keys. Flip back and the other side is exactly as you left it.

Bills

Pay a gift card or a phone top-up from the treasury. The provider's order becomes an ordinary payment: your policy checks it, your council approves it, and it is refused if the order's 15 minutes run out. No late payments to an expired order.

Bank transfers (India)

Rupees in and out of the treasury through the business's own Credible Finance account. Add money by UPI and it arrives as USDC. Cash out is a normal USDC payment your council approves, then Credible pays the rupees. Bank numbers stay with Credible, never us.

Post only after the first real run with Credible (section 5).

Stocks

Over 1,200 US stocks and ETFs on the business's own Backpack account. The treasury funds it with an approved payment, and each trade is capped by the most your council allows for one investment. A limit order only fills within 1% of the price you saw.

Attack lab

We tried to make our own agent drain the treasury. A prompt injection, an invoice that fools the AI into saying "pay", and a clean forgery that fools it again. None was paid: each was stopped by a different layer, and the agent's key can only propose, never send. Try the three yourself in demo. [link]

Attach the Attack lab screenshot. Say it runs in demo mode.

Payroll

Payroll from a spreadsheet: one line per person, each paid in the stablecoin and chain they chose. Every line is checked first (address, sanctions, the shared blocklist), then the council approves the whole run with one vote and NEAR Intents delivers everyone's pay.

Tested in demo and in tests, not yet on mainnet: do not say "teams are paying with it".

Backups

Your treasury's records, backed up encrypted to your own bucket with your own passphrase, every day. Restore is one button and keeps what it replaced.

Tested against a local S3-shaped server, not yet a real bucket. Post after one real run.

Price charts

Open any coin in Bluvfi Business for a chart from one day to one year, next to what the treasury holds. Live prices, no account needed.

Privacy and proofs

Not every business wants every payment public. Privacy in Bluvfi Business is optional and per payment, and where a claim has to be proved (a sanctions check, a passport rule) it can be proved in zero knowledge, so the data behind it is not shared.

Tests

317 automated tests, plus checks that run against the real services (NEAR Intents, NEAR AI, Bitrefill, Backpack, CoinGecko). Where something has not been run for real, the spec says so on the page, not in a footnote.

5. Claims

This is the check before you post. If a claim is not in the first column, do not make it.

You can say

ClaimWhat backs it
The agent's key can only propose a payment.It is a function-call key scoped to add_proposal on the DAO. Council members vote.
No smart contracts of our own.The treasury is a Sputnik DAO, the contract Trezu runs on. Nothing here is deployed by us.
Every decision leaves checkable evidence.Policy versions are hashed, the AI's review is signed, records are signed (including a post-quantum signature), and a Check page re-verifies proofs.
Demo mode needs no keys and no funds, and keeps separate records.Tested: demo and live records are separate for the same business, and demo flows run with the network switched off.
317 automated tests, and live-service tests against NEAR Intents, NEAR AI, Bitrefill, Backpack and CoinGecko.The spec's reproducibility section. Three live tests skip when a service needs a key or blocks the network, and the spec lists them.
In the Attack lab, none of the three attacks is paid, including when the AI is fooled.Run by npm run demo and the tests; the demo fails if any attack is paid. It is a demo: the AI's fooled answers are replayed, not live.
Payroll is paid by one council vote, each person on their own chain.One DAO proposal per 8 people, tested in demo and in the tests. Say "in demo" until it has run on mainnet.
Prices and charts are live; stock prices come from Backpack.Read from the real CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap (when keyed) and Backpack.

You cannot say

Do not claimWhy
"Audited", "secure", "unhackable".No outside audit has been done. Say what is tested instead.
"Works with Indian banks" as a live fact.The rupee flow is built to Credible's API but has not been run against the real Credible: it only answers from allowed IP addresses, and the machine it was built on is not allowed. Say "built for" until the first real run.
"Buy stocks" or "bank transfers" as live today.Funding and trading were tested against a local stand-in. Only prices, the stock list and charts were read from the real Backpack.
"Cryptorefills works".Its catalogue refused the build machine (403) and was not worked around. Ordering was tested against a stand-in.
"Sell back" for coins, or "withdraw" from stocks.Not built. Selling a coin needs the council to sign an intent, and Backpack withdrawals are made in Backpack.
"We hold nothing" about third parties.Credible and Backpack hold the funds in the business's own accounts with them. Our claim is about the treasury and about us.
Returns, yields, "make money", or any investment advice.It is not advice and no result is promised. Never post a price target or a performance number.
Regulatory approval, licences, or "compliant".None is held. Sanctions screening and identity checks are tools the business runs, not a licence.
Customer counts, volumes or "used by".There are none to cite yet. Do not imply any.
"Every page works in demo".Agent chat needs the real NEAR AI model, and identity checks and the card step of the card on-ramp need real accounts.

6. Replies to expect

Short answers that stay inside section 5.

They askReply
Can the AI steal funds?The agent's key can only call add_proposal on the DAO. It can propose a payment; your council has to vote before anything moves, and your policy gate checks it first.
Is it audited?Not yet. What we can show: 317 automated tests, live checks against the real services where they allow it, and a spec that lists what has not been run for real.

7. Before you post

  • Is every claim in the "can say" column, word for word or narrower?
  • Does the screenshot show demo data? If so, say "demo" in the post.
  • No numbers except the test count, and the test count matches the spec's cover date.
  • No reply promises a date, a price or a provider that is not announced.
  • A third party's name (Credible, Backpack, Bitrefill, NEAR) is a fact, not an endorsement: do not tag them or imply they said anything.
